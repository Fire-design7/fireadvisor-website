You're opening a shop, a café, an office or a warehouse. There's the lease, the fit-out, the furniture and the staff to think about. Fire safety usually comes last, often when someone asks "do you have a fire safety file?". Here's what you need, step by step.

**In short**

- Every facility in operation needs a **fire safety file** with orders, instructions and staff training records.
- **Evacuation plans** are required for certain facilities; for shops, restaurants and offices usually when more than 50 people are present.
- **Fire extinguishers** must be in place, accessible and serviced on time.
- If you're **refurbishing or changing the use** of the premises, you also need the fire safety section of the project.

## Step 1: Be clear about what the facility is

The requirements depend on a few things:

- **the use:** shop, restaurant, office, warehouse, hotel, childcare;
- **how many people** will be inside at once, customers and staff;
- **floor area and floor:** ground floor, shopping centre, basement, a floor in an office building;
- **whether you're changing the premises:** new walls, a change of use, a kitchen in a former shop.

If the premises are being refurbished or their use is changing, you need a project with a [fire safety section](/en/uslugi/chast-pozharna-bezopasnost). If the premises already had the same use and you're not changing it, you usually start straight with the operational documents.

## Step 2: Fire safety file and internal rules

The [fire safety file](/en/blog/pozharno-dosie) is the mandatory set of documents showing that fire safety measures are being followed. For a new facility it usually includes:

- orders on how fire safety is organised and who is responsible;
- fire safety instructions;
- records of staff fire safety training;
- documents for installed systems and their maintenance records.

The file is kept on site and updated whenever the staff, activity or premises change.

## Step 3: Evacuation plans

An evacuation plan (with its floor plans) is required depending on the type of facility:

- **regardless of the number of people:** childcare, hospitals, hotels, halls, museums, schools;
- **with more than 50 people at once:** shops, restaurants, banks, administrative buildings with visitors;
- **if a room holds more than 50 people:** offices, production, warehouses.

The plans are drawn from the facility's actual layout and displayed in visible places. Full details: [Evacuation plans and schemes](/en/blog/shemi-za-evakuatsia).

## Step 4: Fire extinguishers

The number and type of extinguishers you need depend on the floor area and activity. What matters is that they:

- are visible and accessible, with no goods or furniture in front of them;
- have valid servicing;
- are known to your staff, who know where they are and how to use them.

## Step 5: Systems: fire alarm, lighting and more

Depending on the type and size of the facility, you may need:

- a [fire alarm system](/en/uslugi/pozharoizvestyavane);
- [emergency lighting](/en/uslugi/evakuatsionno-osvetlenie) and exit signs;
- in larger facilities: smoke and heat extraction or fire suppression systems.

If the systems are already installed in the premises, check that they work and have a maintenance contract with records. If they're being installed now, the requirements come from the project.

## Shop, restaurant or office: what to watch out for

- **Shop:** goods often pile up in front of exits and extinguishers. Escape routes must be clear at all times.
- **Restaurant with a kitchen:** the kitchen is where the risk is highest. Pay attention to the appliances, ventilation and a suitable extinguisher next to them. The capacity of the dining room determines whether you need evacuation plans.
- **Office:** usually lower risk, but if there's a meeting room or space for more than 50 people, the evacuation requirements change.

## Checklist before opening

- Do you know the use and capacity of the facility?
- Is there a fire safety file with orders and instructions?
- Has staff training been carried out and recorded?
- Do you need evacuation plans, and are they on the walls?
- Are exits clear, and do the doors unlock easily?
- Are there extinguishers with valid servicing in visible places?
- If there's a fire alarm, does it work, and is someone maintaining it?
- If you're refurbishing, do you have the fire safety section of the project?
